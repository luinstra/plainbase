The Plainbase name and the Plainbase logo (`assets/brand/`) are trademarks of
luinstra. The code is licensed under Apache-2.0, and that license deliberately
does **not** grant trademark rights (see section 6 of the [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
This policy explains what you can do with the name without asking.

The canonical home of Plainbase is <https://plainbase.sh> and
<https://github.com/luinstra/plainbase>.

## You can, without permission

- Use the code for anything the Apache-2.0 license allows, including
  commercially. This policy is about the *name*, never the code.
- Refer to the software truthfully as Plainbase ("built on Plainbase",
  "powered by Plainbase", "compatible with Plainbase").
- Redistribute unmodified copies or packages of Plainbase under the
  Plainbase name, stating where it comes from.
- Fork the repository. Keeping the name in an unmodified GitHub fork is fine.

## You need permission (i.e. please don't)

- Offer a hosted, managed, or otherwise commercial service under the Plainbase
  name or logo, or a name confusingly similar to it.
- Distribute a *modified* version under the Plainbase name or logo in a way
  that suggests it is official. Ship your fork under your own name and note
  that it is "a fork of Plainbase".
- Register domains, company names, product names, or social handles that
  imply affiliation with or endorsement by the Plainbase project.
